# Visual Reference Package: Image-Generation Prompts

**No images were generated.** No image-generation tool was available, so these are prompts for you to run in your own tool (Midjourney, SDXL/Flux, DALL·E, etc.) or to give to concept artists.

AI output is **reference only**. Every image needs review against real Omani photo references before it guides any 3D work (see the clothing checklist at the end). Generated images also tend to drift toward generic Gulf dress, so expect corrections.

---

## A. Shared style block (append to every prompt)
> photorealistic Unreal Engine 5 concept art, cinematic natural lighting, physically based materials, realistic human proportions, muted desaturated palette with warm stone and dusk-blue tones, subtle atmospheric haze, 35mm lens, high detail, no text, no logos, no watermark

Negative / avoid:
> cartoon, fantasy armour, oversaturated, generic desert bazaar, Arabian Nights costume, turban styles from other countries, weapons in hand, brand logos, distorted hands

## B. 3D world map
**B1 — Oman world map (game-world overview)**
> 3D tabletop-style terrain model of a geographically inspired Sultanate of Oman seen from a high oblique angle. The Hajar mountain range runs as a rugged arc from the Musandam peninsula in the north down to the Sur coast. The narrow Al Batinah coastal plain sits along the Gulf of Oman, with the wide gravel interior and the Sharqiya sand dunes in the east. The Dhofar mountains in the south are green with monsoon mist above Salalah. Deep wadis cut into the mountains; thin road networks with tiny glowing towns. Realistic elevation, ocean with depth gradient, soft clouds, game-map cartography style with subtle region borders, no labels.

*Check:* shape the coastline from real map data (e.g. OpenStreetMap, SRTM elevation) before building it as a 3D asset. A generated image is not geographic data.

## E. Clothing accuracy checklist (verify before modelling)
- [ ] Dishdasha: Omani collarless cut, single tassel (furakha), colour range, and how it differs from Emirati and Saudi thobes
- [ ] Kumma: embroidery patterns, how it is worn, and its pairing with the dishdasha
- [ ] Mussar: fabric (wool or cotton), wrapping styles, and when it is worn vs. the kumma
- [ ] Khanjar and shal (belt): formal occasions only, and the correct position on the body
- [ ] Women's clothing: urban contemporary vs. regional traditional dress in Ad Dakhiliyah, Dhofar, and Al Batinah
- [ ] Get sign-off from at least one Omani cultural reviewer for every character sheet
