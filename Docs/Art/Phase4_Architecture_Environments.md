# OMAN: THE DARK SIDE — Art Phase 4
Omani architecture collection and environments

**Two parts:**
- **Part A. Environments:** 7 separate 16:9 scenes, at eye level, like a game establishing shot.
- **Part B. Architecture kit:** 10 separate reference images of building elements. These are what a 3D artist will model from.

**Rules:**
- One prompt makes one image.
- Places are "inspired by" real regions, not copies of specific real buildings.
- No text in the images.

## Tips
- **For a consistent look,** generate A1 first, then attach it as a style reference (`--sref` in Midjourney) to the other environment images.
- **Part B is meant for a 3D artist.** For each element, also ask for a close-up and a side view.
- **If the AI adds Arabic words on signs or doors,** they will be garbled. Add `--no writing, calligraphy`.
- **If the buildings look like a different country** (for example Moroccan or Indian domes), say "flat-roofed Omani earth-plastered houses" and add `--no domes`. Mosques are the exception.
