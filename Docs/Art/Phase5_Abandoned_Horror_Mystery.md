# OMAN: THE DARK SIDE — Art Phase 5
Abandoned, horror and mystery locations

**Horror direction:** psychological, not gory.
- Fear comes from emptiness, silence, something slightly wrong, and light in the wrong place.
- No blood pools, no bodies, no monsters, no jump-scare faces.
- No mosques or graves are used as horror settings.
- All locations are **fictional**; none is a real abandoned village or a real crime site.

Every location gets separate images (exterior, interior, detail), all 16:9.

## Tips
- **For a consistent look across this phase,** generate 1A first, then use it as a style reference (`--sref`) for the rest.
- **If the AI adds blood or a monster,** strengthen the `--no` list, and add `empty, quiet, nobody` to the prompt.
- **For 1B and 5A, use Rashid's image from Phase 1 as a character reference,** so the protagonist matches.
- **Uniforms and vehicles are fictional:** no real police logos or insignia.
