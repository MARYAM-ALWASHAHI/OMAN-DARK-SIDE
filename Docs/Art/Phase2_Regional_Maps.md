# OMAN: THE DARK SIDE — Art Phase 2
Regional 3D maps (7 regions)

Every region has **two separate images**:
- **A. Day map:** isometric 3D diorama, 16:9, no text.
- **B. Night "dark side" map:** the same region at night. Attach image A as an image reference so the layout stays the same.

**How to read each region:**
- **Real geography** describes the real place, to keep the map believable. Check it against a real map or photos.
- **Game role** and **hidden locations** are fiction. Real place names stay for the regions only; specific haunted or crime locations are invented and renamed, never real sites.
- **Rule:** no real military areas, ports' restricted zones, private homes or real abandoned villages are used as crime or horror locations.

**Shared ending.** In every A prompt, replace `[SHARED ENDING]` with this text:
```
elevated isometric 3D open-world game region map, high oblique camera, tabletop diorama realism, photorealistic terrain and materials, realistic scale of buildings and roads, Unreal Engine 5 quality, volumetric atmosphere, high detail, sharp focus --ar 16:9 --style raw --no text, letters, labels, watermark, flat map, low poly, cartoon, fantasy
```
**Shared night prompt** (B), with `[REGION]` replaced by the region's name:
```
Same [REGION] 3D game region map as the reference image, at night under moonlight: cool blue light on the terrain, warm lights in inhabited areas, darkness and low fog in remote valleys, two or three faint red glowing markers at isolated abandoned spots, subtle and eerie, realistic not fantasy. photorealistic, Unreal Engine 5 quality --ar 16:9 --style raw --no text, labels, watermark, cartoon, low poly
```

## 1. Muscat — the modern capital
**Real geography:**
- A long coastal city squeezed between the Gulf of Oman and dark, jagged rocky mountains.
- Small sheltered coves, a curved waterfront promenade (corniche) with an old market quarter behind it, and a busy harbour.
- Low white buildings with arches and crenellated rooflines, wide palm-lined highways, and residential districts spreading into the valleys between the mountains.

**Game role:** the start of the urban investigations. The police headquarters (fictional), the hospital, the newspaper office where Nora works, and night crime cases.

**Hidden locations (fictional):** a closed underground car park beneath an unfinished tower; an abandoned old house in a narrow cove, reachable only on foot; a maintenance tunnel under the corniche.

### 1A. Muscat day
```
Muscat-inspired modern Omani capital region on the Gulf of Oman: a long coastal city of low white and cream buildings with arched windows and crenellated rooftops, tightly squeezed between the sea and dark jagged brown-grey rocky mountains, small sheltered coves with turquoise water, a curved palm-lined waterfront promenade with an old market quarter behind it, a busy harbour with traditional wooden dhows and modern ships, wide highways with interchanges winding through mountain valleys, residential districts climbing into the valleys, a grand white mosque with a gold dome as one landmark, late afternoon golden light, light sea haze, [SHARED ENDING]
```

## 2. Sohar — the industrial coast
**Real geography:**
- On the flat Al Batinah coastal plain, with a long straight coastline and wide beaches.
- A large modern industrial port with cranes, tanks and container stacks.
- Date palm farms and older villages inland, a historic white fort near the old town, and the Hajar mountains as a faint line far inland.

**Game role:** industrial incidents, smuggling cases, an abandoned workers' camp.

**Hidden locations (fictional):** a shut-down factory with flooded basements; an empty workers' housing block; a fishing village at the end of a sand road.

### 2A. Sohar day
```
Sohar-inspired Omani industrial coastal region on a flat coastal plain: a long straight sandy coastline, a large modern industrial port with gantry cranes, storage tanks and container stacks, a historic white fort near a small old town with low houses, wide straight highways, large green date palm farms and small villages inland, a few abandoned warehouses and an empty workers' camp at the edge of the industrial zone, the Hajar mountains as a faint hazy line far in the distance, hot hazy midday light softening to afternoon, [SHARED ENDING]
```

## 3. Nizwa — the historic interior (the game's first region)
**Real geography:**
- A historic inland town at the foot of the Hajar mountains, known for its massive round fort tower and traditional market.
- Old mud-brick quarters (harat) with narrow lanes, palm oases watered by aflaj channels, and dry wadis.
- Brown mountains rising to the north.

**Game role:** chapter 1. The fictional town Wadi Al-Sukoon and Harat Al-Ramad are "inspired by" this region, not copies of real Nizwa.

**Hidden locations (fictional):** Harat Al-Ramad (the abandoned quarter); the eighth house; the old well; the abandoned clinic; a cave in the ridge.

### 3A. Nizwa day
```
Nizwa-inspired historic Omani interior region: a traditional inland town at the foot of rugged brown mountains, a massive round stone fort tower as the central landmark beside a traditional market with arcades, old mud-brick quarters with narrow lanes and carved wooden doors, dense green date palm oases fed by narrow stone irrigation channels (aflaj), dry rocky wadis winding out of the mountains, an abandoned crumbling mud-brick quarter on a slope at the edge of town, gravel tracks to small villages, warm golden-hour light with long shadows, [SHARED ENDING]
```

## 4. Jebel Akhdar — the mountain plateau
**Real geography:**
- A high limestone plateau of roughly 2,000 m in the central Hajar, reached by a steep switchback road.
- In reality, a police checkpoint only lets 4x4 vehicles up this road.
- Villages cling to canyon edges, with stone terraces of roses, pomegranates and walnuts.
- Deep gorges below, and cold, misty winters.

**Game role:** chapter 3: Salem's village, the terraces, lights seen from above, and dangerous roads.

**Hidden locations (fictional):** a terraced village abandoned after a landslide (invented, not a real abandoned village); a shepherd's cave above the gorge; a collapsed old irrigation tunnel.

### 4A. Jebel Akhdar day
```
Jebel Akhdar-inspired Omani mountain plateau region at about 2000 metres: a high limestone plateau with deep dramatic canyons and gorges, small stone villages clinging to the canyon edges, stepped green agricultural terraces with rose bushes, pomegranate and walnut trees, a steep winding switchback mountain road with crash barriers climbing from the hot plain below, juniper trees and rocky slopes, one small abandoned terraced village on a ledge, cool clear morning light with mist rising out of the gorges, [SHARED ENDING]
```

## 5. Salalah and Dhofar — the green monsoon south
**Real geography:**
- On the Arabian Sea in the far south.
- During the khareef monsoon (about June to September), the coastal mountains turn green and are covered in mist and drizzle.
- Coconut and banana plantations, wide beaches, seasonal waterfalls on the escarpment, camels grazing on green hills, and frankincense trees in the drier valleys.

**Game role:** the seasonal mystery chapter: fog, waterfalls, disappearances during the khareef.

**Hidden locations (fictional):** an abandoned farmhouse inside the mist; a cave behind a seasonal waterfall; an empty tourist rest house closed since the off-season.

### 5A. Salalah day (khareef)
```
Salalah and Dhofar-inspired southern Omani region during the monsoon season: a coastal city of low white buildings on the Arabian Sea with long sandy beaches and coconut and banana plantations, behind it a steep escarpment of rolling mountains covered in vivid green grass and low trees, thick drifting mist and soft drizzle over the hills, seasonal waterfalls on limestone cliffs, camels grazing on green slopes, winding mountain roads disappearing into fog, a few frankincense trees in a drier valley, soft overcast diffused light, [SHARED ENDING]
```

## 6. Sharqiya Sands — the eastern desert
**Real geography:**
- A large sand sea in the east of the country, made of long parallel dune ridges in shades of orange and red.
- Small Bedouin settlements and desert camps at its edges.
- Green palm oases in nearby wadis, and long straight desert roads along the margins.

**Game role:** night survival missions, Nora's camp, the dune chase, lost signals.

**Hidden locations (fictional):** a half-buried abandoned camp; the wreck of an old vehicle in the dunes; a sealed water cistern.

### 6A. Sharqiya Sands day
```
Sharqiya Sands-inspired Omani eastern desert region: a vast sea of long parallel orange and red sand dune ridges stretching to the horizon, small desert camps with tents and parked 4x4 vehicles at the edge of the dunes, a few low houses of a Bedouin settlement, a green palm oasis in a nearby rocky wadi with turquoise pools, a long straight desert highway along the edge, tyre tracks crossing the sand, one half-buried abandoned camp deep in the dunes, warm low sunset light raking across the dunes, [SHARED ENDING]
```

## 7. Musandam — the northern fjords
**Real geography:**
- A separate peninsula in the far north, cut off from the rest of Oman by UAE territory.
- Steep, bare limestone mountains drop straight into deep blue sea inlets (khors), often described as "fjords of Arabia".
- Small isolated villages are reached mainly by boat, with traditional wooden dhows, and a mountain road climbs high above the sea.

**Game role:** isolated villages, sea caves, missions reached by boat. A late chapter or expansion.

**Hidden locations (fictional):** a village empty for decades on a narrow shore; a sea cave that floods at high tide; a lighthouse that no longer works.

**Note:** do not include real military or restricted coastal sites in the game.

### 7A. Musandam day
```
Musandam-inspired northern Omani fjord region: steep bare layered limestone mountains plunging straight into deep blue and turquoise sea inlets, narrow fjord-like bays, small isolated fishing villages on tiny strips of shore at the base of the cliffs, traditional wooden dhows on the water, a winding mountain road high above the sea, a dark sea cave entrance at water level, one tiny abandoned village on a narrow beach, bright clear morning light with deep shadows in the inlets, [SHARED ENDING]
```

## Tips
- **Generate all seven A maps first,** then pick the ones that look like they belong to the same game: same lighting style and the same camera height.
- **If a map looks like a postcard or a painting,** add `game world map, isometric, diorama` to the start of the prompt.
- **If the AI invents fantasy castles or skyscrapers,** add `--no castle, skyscraper` (or for Musandam: `--no city`).
